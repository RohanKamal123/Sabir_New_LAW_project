02 — The problem

The work is repetitive, and the mistakes are expensive.

Four tasks repeat every single working week. Each is easy to do badly and costly to get wrong — ordered here by how much a mistake costs.

The costly one

Watch the deadline

Filing deadlines are set by law, not by the judge. Miss one and the client's cause of action can be barred entirely — the most expensive mistake in practice.

Missed deadline = cause of action BARRED · Tk 500K+ fee loss
Task 1

Check the cause list

Every evening the Court publishes the roster of tomorrow's hearings. The barrister must find their own matters across ~60 benches — at 9pm, the night before.

A missed listing = default + a wasted day + a fee lost
Task 4

Draft correctly

A petition has a fixed, conventional shape. Drafting is slow, and a wrong citation or a wrong section can sink a matter you'll only regret later.

A wrong citation = a permanent loss of trust
Task 2

Track the case

Old matters move slowly. A result — an order, an adjournment, a judgment — can appear on a case you'd stopped watching. Someone has to re-check the history.

A missed result = months of wasted re-checking

The Court offers no API. The list is plain HTML, published the evening before. The statute text is free. Nothing exists to tie it all together — until now.

05 — Why you can trust it

Built to refuse, so it earns trust.

The credibility of practice software rests on one thing: the barrister believing the red things when they appear. So this product is built around what it refuses to do — and each refusal is paired with its proof.

▲ No invented case law

Even the big global tools invent citations 17–33% of the time. This tool refuses rather than risk a false citation — search a case, get "not found," and that is the right answer.

▲ No unverified rules

Limitation articles were extracted by machine; none is verified yet, and every one stays refused until a lawyer checks it.

▲ No generated answers

It never answers with a Bangladeshi judgment from memory, and it never writes statutory text. What it returns is verbatim, with the source attached.

▲ No invented citations

It refuses to cite any authority you did not supply, and it flags unverified citations for your review.

The proof beside each refusal

Statute text is exact spans from the official Bangladesh Code, with the URL. Rules are rule-based date arithmetic — sections 4, 5 and 12 as exact law, not model memory.

What is shown, and why

A limitation figure you cannot audit is a figure you cannot file on. Every step, every citation, every exclusion is laid out — and it prints correctly, so you can take it to court.

DATA · Your data is not our data

The cause list is read from the Court's public pages; statute text from the Bangladesh Code. Your files, matters and time entries live on your machine or your server — no cloud, no sync, no analytics. If you run it locally, nobody but you sees your files.
